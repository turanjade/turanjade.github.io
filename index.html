Hi, I'm Ran Tu.

Researcher · Engineer · Founder of TreesLab

I'm a transportation researcher and engineer based in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. I work at NCTCOG as a Transportation Modeler, supporting regional land use & mobility plans and traffic management strategies.

My research centers on traffic emission and energy modelling, traffic-related air quality, and green transport strategy. I founded TreesLab to push toward a sustainable, efficient, and eco-friendly future for transportation.

I'm always open to collaboration on data analysis, engineering consulting, and academic research — get in touch!
